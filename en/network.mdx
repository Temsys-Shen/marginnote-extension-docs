title: "Network restrictions"
---

# Connection scope

The add-on panel allows `https:` and `wss:` connections, plus `http:` and `ws:` connections to loopback addresses on the local machine.

Local connections are limited to the following addresses, and any port is allowed:

- `http://localhost:<port>`
- `http://127.0.0.1:<port>`
- `ws://localhost:<port>`
- `ws://127.0.0.1:<port>`

Non-local `http:` and `ws:` connections, as well as `0.0.0.0`, `::1`, LAN IPs, and custom hostnames, are all blocked.

# Cross-origin requests

The panel's origin is `mnpanel://panel`. You may need this value when configuring CORS on your server:

```text
Access-Control-Allow-Origin: mnpanel://panel
```

# Navigation

- When the user clicks an `http:` or `https:` link, the address is handed to the system browser and the panel does not navigate.
- Changing `location` from the page, or submitting a form, has no effect. Use `fetch` to send data to a server.

# Content security policy

Every panel response carries a content security policy (CSP) that limits the resources the page can load and the connections it can make.

- Loading: same-origin panel resources, `mnpanel:`, `https:`, and HTTP resources from `localhost` and `127.0.0.1`. This covers scripts, styles, images, fonts, audio/video, and iframes.
- Connections: same-origin panel resources, `mnpanel:`, `https:`, `wss:`, local HTTP, local WebSocket, `data:`, and `blob:`.
- Additional sources: images and media may also use `data:` and `blob:`; fonts may also use `data:`.
- Blocked: `object` tags, `<base>` tags, and form submission.

Each directive lists its sources explicitly. The complete policy is:

```text
default-src 'self' mnpanel: https: http://localhost:* http://127.0.0.1:*
connect-src 'self' mnpanel: https: wss: http://localhost:* http://127.0.0.1:* ws://localhost:* ws://127.0.0.1:* data: blob:
script-src 'self' mnpanel: https: http://localhost:* http://127.0.0.1:* 'unsafe-inline' 'unsafe-eval'
style-src 'self' mnpanel: https: http://localhost:* http://127.0.0.1:* 'unsafe-inline'
img-src 'self' mnpanel: https: http://localhost:* http://127.0.0.1:* data: blob:
media-src 'self' mnpanel: https: http://localhost:* http://127.0.0.1:* data: blob:
font-src 'self' mnpanel: https: http://localhost:* http://127.0.0.1:* data:
frame-src 'self' mnpanel: https: http://localhost:* http://127.0.0.1:*
object-src 'none'
base-uri 'none'
form-action 'none'
```
